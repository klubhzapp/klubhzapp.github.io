Privacy

Klubhz is a private college basketball board for friends. This page explains what it keeps.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile photo. We store the display name and photo you choose, the teams you follow, the bets you log, and messages you post in game chats.

How it is used

Only to run the site: to show your profile, keep your bet history and record, and show chat messages to other signed-in members. Your logged bets are visible only to you. Chat messages and your display name are visible to other members.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your information, show ads, or use it for anything outside Klubhz. Klubhz does not take wagers or handle money.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase. Sign-in is handled by Google.

Deleting your data

You can delete your own chat messages any time. To delete your account and everything tied to it, ask the person who invited you.
